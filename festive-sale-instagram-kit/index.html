FAQ

Do I need Canva Pro?

No. The kit uses only free Canva fonts (Playfair Display, Poppins, Anton), free elements and free photo frames. A free Canva account on web or mobile can open, edit and download every page without a payment prompt.

What exactly do I receive after payment?

A download with the 7-page PDF guide, licence and README. The guide contains the Canva template links (one per design: Feed Posts and Stories); open each, click Use template and the 30 pages are copied into your own Canva account. You do not receive flat image files, you edit the designs and download the pages you need.

Can I change the colours, fonts, dates and prices?

Yes, everything is editable text and shapes. Each festival has its own palette saved in the design, with hex codes in the guide, and the layouts also work with your brand colours. Dates, percentages, codes and prices are plain text placeholders.

Can I use it for client accounts or more than one business?

You can use the templates for accounts you own or manage, including client accounts, with no limit on posts. You may not share the template link or resell the templates or exported designs as templates. One purchase per person or business.

Will it still be useful after this season?

Yes. Years and dates are plain text, and the countdown, offer, product spotlight and last-day layouts work for any sale. Duplicate a page, swap the palette and reuse it for Valentine's, Holi or your anniversary sale.
