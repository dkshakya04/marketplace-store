03 — About

A small firm with a big-company toolkit.

We started in 2012 as two consultants and a shared desk, convinced that mid-size companies deserved the same quality of strategic thinking that large enterprises take for granted.

Today we are a team of thirty-two strategists, designers, engineers and analysts working from three cities. We have launched products in healthcare, finance, logistics and education, and we still run every engagement the same way: a short diagnostic, a fixed-scope plan, weekly numbers and a hand-over your team can actually run with.

We measure ourselves on one thing: whether your business is noticeably better twelve months after we met. That is why most of our work comes from referrals and why many clients have been with us for over five years.

Good strategy is simple to explain and hard to copy. Our job is to find yours and make it visible everywhere your customers look.Aarav Mehta, Managing Partner

08 — FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

Something else on your mind? Send us a note and a real person will reply within one business day.

Ask a question
How long does a typical engagement take?

A brand or website project usually runs eight to twelve weeks from kick-off to launch. Retainers start with a two-week diagnostic and then continue month to month.

Do you work with companies outside your own country?

Yes. Roughly half of our clients are in other time zones. We schedule a weekly overlap window and keep everything else asynchronous in a shared workspace.

What do you need from us to get started?

A decision-maker who can attend the weekly call, access to your existing analytics and brand files, and about two hours of stakeholder time during the diagnostic.

Can we start small and expand later?

Absolutely. Most retainers begin with a single service line. You can add services or upgrade your plan at any monthly review.

Who owns the work when the project ends?

You do. All strategy documents, design files, code and accounts are handed over in your name, with documentation and a recorded walkthrough.
