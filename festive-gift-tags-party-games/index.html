FAQ

What paper size and printer do I need?

The PDF is A4 portrait. Any home inkjet or laser printer works; print at 100% (actual size), not "fit to page". If you only have Letter paper, choose "fit to printable area" and the sheets scale down slightly. A local print shop can print it as-is.

Can I edit the text or add names?

This is a print-ready PDF, not an editable file. Every tag has To/From lines and the blank-tag sheet has an empty message line, so you write names by hand. A personalised-names add-on (a custom tag sheet with your names printed) is available as a premium tier where offered.

Are the Tambola tickets really unique?

Yes. All 12 tickets follow the standard 3 x 9 Housie layout with 15 numbers each and different number sets, so up to 12 players can play from one print-out. Print the sheets twice for a bigger group and split into two rounds.

Is this suitable for an office Secret Santa?

Yes. Pages 14-18 are a complete office kit: draw slips, a host tracking sheet, question and dare cards (all office-safe) and a Guess the Gift score sheet. Print one slip sheet per 12 participants.
