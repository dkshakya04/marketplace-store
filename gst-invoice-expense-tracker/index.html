About this product

One Excel / Google Sheets workbook for self-employed professionals and small businesses in India that turns invoices and bills into a live dashboard, GST summary with filing dates, ITC, TDS and an advance-tax estimate for FY 2026-27.

Stop keeping invoices in one file, bills in another and GST in your head. This single .xlsx workbook (opens in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice, no macros) records every invoice and expense once and does the rest: CGST + SGST or IGST splits itself from the client's state, TDS deducted by clients is tracked, overdue invoices turn red, input tax credit is totalled against the GST you collected, and a dashboard shows what you earned, spent, are owed and roughly what advance tax is coming on 15 December and 15 March.

What the 9 sheets do

  • Settings: business details, GSTIN, state, GST registered Yes/No toggle (businesses below the registration threshold can use it too), Monthly or QRMP filing scheme, FY start, currency label, default payment terms, estimated tax rate, bank and UPI details.
  • Dashboard: invoiced, received, outstanding, overdue, TDS deducted, expenses, profit, GST collected vs ITC and net GST; month-by-month table for April 2026 to March 2027; advance-tax estimate with the 15 Jun / 15 Sep / 15 Dec / 15 Mar schedule (15% / 45% / 75% / 100%); outstanding by client.
  • Invoices (500 ready rows): invoice number, date, client dropdown, SAC code, taxable value, GST rate dropdown (0/5/12/18/28%), automatic CGST/SGST or IGST, invoice total, optional TDS 194J column, net receivable, due date from the client's payment terms, Paid / Unpaid / Overdue status with red highlighting.
  • Clients: name, GSTIN, state or country dropdown with automatic state code, contact, billing address, payment terms.
  • Expenses (1,000 ready rows): date, category dropdown, vendor, amount before GST, rate, GST, bill total, ITC eligible Yes/No, ITC claimable, net cost.
  • GST Summary: month-wise and quarter-wise output tax, ITC and net payable, GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B / PMT-06 due dates for your scheme and state, GSTR-9 and record-keeping reminders.
  • Invoice Template: pick an invoice number from a dropdown and a one-page A4 tax invoice fills in (supplier, client GSTIN, place of supply, SAC, GST split, TDS note, bank details). Export to PDF and send.
  • Tax Notes: plain-language explanations of GST registration, CGST/SGST vs IGST, ITC rules, QRMP, advance tax, section 44ADA, TDS 194J, invoice rules and the FY 2026-27 calendar.
  • Lists: the dropdown sources (GST rates, 36 state/UT codes plus Overseas and Other Territory, with QRMP due days, expense categories, SAC codes) - editable.

How it works

Fill the yellow cells on Settings, add your clients, delete the clearly marked sample rows and start typing invoices and bills. Grey cells are formulas; totals read whole columns, so new rows are picked up automatically. Standard functions only (SUMIFS, INDEX/MATCH, EDATE, TEXT), so nothing breaks when you import into Google Sheets. The included setup guide (PDF) covers import, every column, a SAC and GST-rate cheat-sheet and the FY 2026-27 filing calendar.

This is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice. Rates, thresholds and due dates reflect October 2026; verify with your CA before filing.

FAQ

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Open sheets.google.com, File > Import > Upload the .xlsx and choose "Replace spreadsheet". Dropdowns, conditional formatting, frozen headers and named ranges come across. The guide has step-by-step instructions, and the workbook uses only standard functions that Sheets, Excel 2016+ and LibreOffice all support.

I am not registered under GST yet. Is it still useful?

Yes. Set GST registered to No on the Settings sheet. GST and ITC columns stay at zero, the invoice prints as a plain invoice, and you still get the invoice log, expense log, overdue tracking, dashboard and advance-tax estimate. Flip the toggle to Yes when you register.

How does it know whether to charge CGST+SGST or IGST?

Each client has a state or country on the Clients sheet. The workbook compares the client's state code with yours (from Settings). Same state gives CGST + SGST split equally; a different state or an overseas client gives IGST. You choose the rate (0% for exports under LUT, 18% for most services).

Is the advance-tax figure accurate?

It is an estimate, not a computation. The Dashboard projects your profit from the months elapsed so far, applies the effective tax rate you enter on Settings, subtracts TDS already deducted by clients and spreads the result over the four instalment dates. Use it as an early warning and confirm the real amount with your CA.

Can I add more invoices than the 500 ready rows, or my own expense categories?

Yes. Copy the last ready row down and the formulas extend; totals read whole columns so nothing else needs changing. Expense categories and SAC codes live on the Lists sheet and can be edited.

Is this tax advice, and is it up to date?

No, it is a record-keeping tool with plain-language notes. Rates, thresholds and due dates reflect the rules as understood in October 2026; Budgets and GST Council decisions change them, so verify with a chartered accountant and the official portals before filing.
